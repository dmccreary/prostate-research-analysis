# Comprehensive Paper-by-Paper Audit and Ground Truth Evaluation of `labeled-dataset-v2.csv`

## 1. Objective and Purpose of the Analysis

The objective of this analysis is to perform an exhaustive, record-by-record clinical and methodological audit of all **524 articles** in [`data/labeled-dataset-v2.csv`](../data/labeled-dataset-v2.csv) against the **Prostate Cancer Results Study Group (PCRSG) Article Inclusion Criteria**. Specifically, this evaluation:
1. Examines every single paper individually based strictly on its **Title** and **Abstract** (without skipping any records or assuming ground truth labels are correct a priori).
2. Explains why each article was assigned its ground truth label (`1` = Included / Positive, `0` = Excluded / Negative) by mapping its specific study population, treatment modality, risk-group stratification, sample size, follow-up duration, radiation dose, and oncologic survival endpoints against the PCRSG criteria.
3. Evaluates the internal consistency and accuracy of the existing ground truth labels, separating articles into three rigorous diagnostic categories:
   - **Correctly Labeled (`Consistent`)**: The title and abstract provide explicit evidence supporting the assigned ground truth label (`1` or `0`), and the `Labeling Discrepancy` column is left empty.
   - **Potential Labeling Errors and Outliers (`Potential Labeling Error`)**: The title and/or abstract contain explicit clinical or methodological evidence that directly contradicts the assigned ground truth label.
   - **Insufficient Abstract Information (`Insufficient Information`)**: The paper describes a potentially eligible prostate cancer treatment study and survival endpoints without any explicit disqualifying violation in the abstract, but omits one or more mandatory verification parameters (such as median follow-up duration, risk-group sample sizes, pre-treatment risk stratification, or standard EBRT dose) that can only be verified from the full text.

### Deliverables in `dataset_analysis/`
- **[`labeled-dataset-v2-analysis.csv`](./labeled-dataset-v2-analysis.csv)**: Complete 524-row dataset containing `PMID`, `Title`, `Abstract`, `Ground Truth Label`, `Reasoning`, and `Labeling Discrepancy`.
- **[`README.md`](./README.md)**: This comprehensive technical report documenting the criteria, methodology, quantitative summary, outlier catalog, insufficient-information inventory, and structural findings.

## 2. Inclusion Criteria Used for Evaluation

Each paper was evaluated against the official **PCRSG Article Inclusion Criteria**:

### 2.1 Accepted Primary Treatment Modalities
1. **Radical Prostatectomy - Open** (retropubic / perineal)
2. **Radical Prostatectomy - Robotic** (and laparoscopic)
3. **Standard EBRT** (conventional fractionation, minimum 72 Gy)
4. **Hypofractionated EBRT**
5. **Ultrahypofractionated EBRT** (SBRT / SABR)
6. **Protons** (conformal / spot-scanning proton beam therapy)
7. **LDR Brachytherapy** (permanent interstitial seed implantation: I-125, Pd-103, Cs-131)
8. **HDR Brachytherapy** (Ir-192 high-dose-rate brachytherapy)
9. **EBRT + LDR Brachytherapy**
10. **EBRT + HDR Brachytherapy**
11. **ADT + Standard EBRT**
12. **ADT + Hypofractionated EBRT**
13. **ADT + Ultrahypofractionated EBRT**
14. **ADT + LDR Brachytherapy**
15. **ADT + HDR Brachytherapy**
16. **ADT + EBRT + LDR Brachytherapy**
17. **ADT + EBRT + HDR Brachytherapy**
18. **Cryotherapy** (cryoablation / cryosurgery)
19. **HIFU** (High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound)

*Non-accepted treatment settings:* Salvage therapy for recurrent/relapsed disease after prior primary radiation or surgery; postoperative adjuvant/salvage radiotherapy after radical prostatectomy; metastatic (M1/oligometastatic) or castration-resistant prostate cancer (CRPC/mCRPC) systemic regimens; palliative therapy; active surveillance / watchful waiting / observation alone without an evaluated active curative treatment cohort; ADT or chemotherapy monotherapy without definitive local therapy; or purely diagnostic/biopsy/imaging/biomarker studies.

### 2.2 Study Acceptance Criteria
1. **Pre-Treatment Risk Group Stratification & Post-Treatment Maintenance**: Patients must be stratified into recognizable pre-treatment risk groups (**Low**, **Intermediate**, and/or **High Risk**) by **D'Amico**, **Zelefsky**, or **NCCN** stratification (or equivalent pre-treatment clinical T-stage, biopsy Gleason score, and baseline PSA risk tiers), and the risk group classification must be maintained in post-treatment outcome reporting.
2. **Accepted Oncologic Endpoints**: Studies must evaluate and report at least one recognized long-term oncologic survival endpoint:
   - **BRFS** (Biochemical Relapse-Free Survival / biochemical recurrence-free survival / freedom from biochemical failure — ASTRO, Phoenix [nadir + 2 ng/mL], or surgical PSA < 0.2 ng/mL)
   - **OS** (Overall Survival)
   - **MFS** (Metastasis-Free Survival / distant metastasis-free survival)
   - **CSS** (Cause-Specific Survival / prostate cancer-specific survival / disease-specific survival)
3. **No Pathologic Exclusions / No Pathologic Staging Selection**: Staging must be pre-treatment clinical staging. Investigators cannot eliminate or exclude patients after surgery or prior to radiotherapy due to unfavorable post-operative/surgical pathology (such as positive surgical margins, extraprostatic extension, seminal vesicle invasion, or positive pelvic lymph nodes), nor select a cohort exclusively by post-operative pathological stage/grade (e.g., restricted to pathological `pT2`, `pT2R0`, `pT3b-T4`, or surgical specimen Gleason 8-10).
4. **Modern External Beam Radiation Dose**: Standard-fractionation EBRT (IMRT / 3D-CRT) monotherapy (with or without ADT) must deliver a minimum dose of **>= 72 Gy**. *(Note: Lower EBRT doses of 40-50.4 Gy are standard when combined with an LDR or HDR brachytherapy boost, and hypofractionated/ultrahypofractionated SBRT regimens use higher doses per fraction with higher biologically effective doses.)*
5. **Peer-Reviewed Original Research**: Only original clinical research articles published in peer-reviewed journals meet criteria. Narrative reviews, systematic reviews/meta-analyses without primary eligible cohort reporting, editorials, comments, clinical guidelines, trial protocols without outcomes, presentations, and posters are excluded.
6. **Minimum Sample Size by Risk Group**:
   - **Low-Risk**: >= 100 patients
   - **Intermediate-Risk**: >= 100 patients
   - **High-Risk**: >= 50 patients
7. **Minimum Median Follow-Up**: Minimum median follow-up of **5 years (60 months)**.

## 3. Methodology and Approach Followed to Assess Each Paper

Every record (`row_index` 0 to 523) in `data/labeled-dataset-v2.csv` was individually read and evaluated across all eight PCRSG dimensions:

1. **Direct Textual Verification (Zero Template/Label Assumption)**: Each paper's `Title` and `Abstract` (along with PubMed `publication_types` for identifying reviews, editorials, and meta-analyses) were inspected to extract the exact study design, treatment modality, total and subgroup sample sizes ($N$), pre-treatment risk classification system, radiation dose and fractionation (in Gy), median/mean follow-up duration (in months or years), reported survival endpoints (BRFS, OS, MFS, CSS), and any pathological or salvage exclusions.
2. **Mathematical Sample-Size Verification (Pigeonhole Principle for Multi-Risk Cohorts)**: When an abstract reports outcomes stratified across Low-, Intermediate-, and High-Risk groups but states only the total cohort size $N$ rather than individual subgroup headcounts, the worst-case non-qualifying partition is $99$ Low-Risk + $99$ Intermediate-Risk + $49$ High-Risk = **$247$ patients**. By the Pigeonhole Principle, any 3-group risk-stratified cohort with **$N \ge 248$** is mathematically guaranteed to have at least one risk group meeting its PCRSG sample size threshold ($\ge 100$ Low, $\ge 100$ Intermediate, or $\ge 50$ High). Similarly, a 2-group Intermediate + High risk cohort with **$N \ge 149$** ($99 + 49 = 148$) is guaranteed to meet the sample size threshold in at least one group. Conversely, when $N < 248$ across three risk groups (e.g., $N = 100$ or $N = 209$) and subgroup counts are not stated in the abstract, sample-size compliance cannot be mathematically confirmed from the abstract alone.
3. **Three-Tier Discrepancy Classification Rubric**:
   - **`Consistent` (`Labeling Discrepancy` left empty `""`)**:
     - *For Ground Truth Label `1`*: The title and abstract explicitly verify an accepted primary treatment modality, accepted survival endpoint(s) (BRFS/OS/MFS/CSS), pre-treatment risk stratification/maintenance, adequate sample size ($\ge 100$ Low/Intermediate or $\ge 50$ High, directly or via the Pigeonhole Principle), explicit median/mean follow-up $\ge 5$ years (60 months), modern EBRT dose ($\ge 72$ Gy where applicable), and no disqualifying features.
     - *For Ground Truth Label `0`*: The title and abstract explicitly demonstrate failure of at least one mandatory PCRSG inclusion criterion (e.g., narrative review/meta-analysis/editorial/protocol, salvage or post-prostatectomy adjuvant therapy, metastatic/CRPC cohort, toxicity/QoL/functional/dosimetric-only outcomes without survival endpoints, explicit median/mean follow-up $< 60$ months, sample size below minimum thresholds, conventional EBRT dose $< 72$ Gy, or post-operative pathological staging/margin exclusions).
   - **`Potential Labeling Error` (`Labeling Discrepancy` prefixed with `Potential Labeling Error:`)**:
     - *For Ground Truth Label `1` (False Positive / Positive Outlier)*: The title and/or abstract contain **explicit disqualifying evidence** violating a hard PCRSG rule—such as an explicit cohort median/mean follow-up $< 60$ months ($< 5.0$ years) with no subgroup reaching $\ge 60$ months, cohort selection restricted to post-operative pathological stage/grade (`pT2`, `pT2R0`, `pT3b-T4`, or pathological Gleason $\ge 8$) or laparoscopic lymph-node exclusion, conventional EBRT monotherapy dose $< 72$ Gy across the entire cohort, postoperative adjuvant therapy, post-relapse natural history cohorts, narrative reviews, or diagnostic prostate biopsy studies.
     - *For Ground Truth Label `0` (False Negative / Negative Outlier)*: The title and abstract **explicitly satisfy all PCRSG inclusion criteria** (accepted primary treatment, modern radiation dose, explicit pre-treatment risk stratification maintained in outcomes, verified sample size $\ge 100$ Low/Intermediate or $\ge 50$ High, explicit median follow-up $\ge 60$ months, and quantitative BRFS/OS/MFS/CSS endpoints) with no identifiable exclusion criterion.
   - **`Insufficient Information` (`Labeling Discrepancy` prefixed with `Insufficient Information:`)**:
     - *For Ground Truth Label `1`*: The abstract describes an accepted primary prostate cancer treatment and survival endpoints with no explicit disqualifier, but omits one or more critical verification parameters from the abstract text (most commonly median follow-up duration, pre-treatment risk-group stratification/outcomes, EBRT dose in Gy, or subgroup patient counts when total $N$ is below the Pigeonhole threshold).
     - *For Ground Truth Label `0`*: The abstract describes an accepted primary treatment and survival endpoints without any explicit exclusion criterion in the abstract, but omits required quantitative or methodological details (such as median follow-up duration, EBRT dose, or risk-group subgroup counts/stratification) needed to determine whether the article was legitimately excluded upon full-text review or represents an unlabeled positive.

## 5. Analysis of Correctly Labeled Papers ($N = 401$)

A total of **401 out of 524 papers (76.53%)** have ground truth labels that are fully verified and consistent with the PCRSG inclusion criteria based on their titles and abstracts alone.

### 5.1 Correctly Labeled Positive Papers (`Label = 1`, $N = 187$)
- **187 out of 281 positive papers (66.55%)** explicitly satisfy every applicable PCRSG requirement directly within their title and abstract: an accepted curative primary treatment modality, pre-treatment risk group stratification (D'Amico, NCCN, or explicit clinical risk tiers) maintained in outcome reporting, adequate risk-group sample sizes ($\ge 100$ Low/Intermediate or $\ge 50$ High), explicit median/mean follow-up $\ge 60$ months ($\ge 5.0$ years), modern radiation dose ($\ge 72$ Gy for conventional EBRT monotherapy), and quantitative long-term BRFS, OS, MFS, and/or CSS outcomes.
- In the most recent positive cohorts (Rows 212-280, published 2016-2021), **61 out of 69 papers (88.4%)** are fully verifiable from the abstract alone, reflecting modern CONSORT/STROBE structured abstract reporting standards.

### 5.2 Correctly Labeled Negative Papers (`Label = 0`, $N = 214$)
- **214 out of 243 negative papers (88.07%)** contain explicit, unambiguous disqualifying criteria in their titles and/or abstracts. Note that many excluded papers fail multiple PCRSG criteria simultaneously:
  - **Salvage, Post-Relapse, or Postoperative Adjuvant Therapy Setting ($n = 71$)**: Studies evaluating salvage cryotherapy, salvage HIFU, salvage brachytherapy, salvage radical prostatectomy after radiation failure, or immediate postoperative adjuvant/salvage radiotherapy after radical prostatectomy (e.g., EORTC 22911 in PMIDs `16099293` and `16223581`, SWOG/ARO trials, or prostate-bed SBRT).
  - **Explicit Follow-Up $< 5$ Years / $< 60$ Months ($n = 59$)**: Primary treatment studies whose reported cohort median or mean follow-up is explicitly below 60 months (e.g., 12 to 54 months).
  - **Non-Original Study Design / Publication Type ($n = 51$)**: Narrative literature reviews, systematic reviews and meta-analyses without primary cohort eligibility, editorial comments (including PMID `21056265`, which has no abstract in PubMed but is explicitly titled *"Editorial comment... --a literature review"*), clinical guidelines, and phase III clinical trial protocols without outcome data (e.g., PMID `16086035`).
  - **Sample Size Below Risk-Group Thresholds ($n = 44$)**: Studies with total sample size $N < 50$ (or $< 100$ in low/intermediate-risk cohorts) or explicit risk-group headcounts below PCRSG minimums.
  - **Absence of Accepted Oncologic Survival Endpoints ($n = 43$)**: Studies reporting only acute/late GU/GI toxicity, quality of life (EPIC/PROs), erectile/urinary function, radiation dosimetry, PSA bounce/kinetics, or surgical learning curves without BRFS, OS, MFS, or CSS.
  - **Post-Operative Pathological Staging or Surgical Exclusions ($n = 30$)**: Studies selecting patients by surgical specimen Gleason score (`pGS 8-10`), `pT3`/`pN+` pathology, or laparoscopic pelvic lymph-node dissection exclusions (e.g., PMID `15774239`).
  - **Metastatic, Oligometastatic, or Castration-Resistant (CRPC) Disease ($n = 15$)**: Studies of mCRPC systemic chemotherapy/radium-223 or metastasis-directed SBRT (e.g., ORIOLE trial, PMID `32215577`).
  - **Sub-Modern Conventional EBRT Dose $< 72$ Gy ($n = 12$)**: Conventional EBRT studies delivering 60-70 Gy without a brachytherapy boost or high-dose comparison arm.

## 8. Overall Observations and Key Findings on Ground Truth Consistency

Our systematic review of all 524 papers reveals several important structural insights regarding the construction and consistency of `labeled-dataset-v2.csv`:

1. **Severe Non-Prostate-Treatment Outlier in the Positive Set (`PMID 30835628`)**:
   - Row 255 (`PMID 30835628`, *"The Ratio of the Number of Biopsy Specimens to Prostate Volume (Biopsy Density) Greater Than 1.5 Improves the Prostate Cancer Detection Rate in Men Undergoing Transperineal Biopsy of the Prostate"*, Stone et al., *J Urol* 2019) is a purely **diagnostic transperineal prostate mapping biopsy study** with zero cancer treatment, zero follow-up, and zero survival outcomes. It entered `labeled-dataset-v2.csv` via `data/accepted_articles_unique.csv` (Paper ID 546), likely due to an author-search or PMID transcription mix-up (as Dr. Nelson N. Stone authored numerous accepted prostate brachytherapy papers in the dataset).
2. **Narrative Review Outlier in the Positive Set (`PMID 19111801`)**:
   - Row 52 (`PMID 19111801`, *"Outcomes for intermediate risk prostate cancer: are there advantages for surgery, external radiation, or brachytherapy?"*) is indexed by PubMed as a `Review` and its abstract explicitly states *"We review recent results... The results suggest similar short-term survival but domain-specific effects on QOL"*, containing no primary cohort numbers, no follow-up duration, and no quantitative long-term survival rates.
3. **Cross-Dataset Labeling Inconsistencies Across Companion Publications of the Same Trial**:
   - **EORTC Trial 22911 (Postoperative Adjuvant Radiotherapy after Radical Prostatectomy)**:
     - The 2005 Lancet primary report (`PMID 16099293`, Row 313) and 2005 European Urology subgroup report (`PMID 16223581`, Row 324) are both labeled **`0` (Negative)** because EORTC 22911 evaluates immediate postoperative irradiation (60 Gy to the surgical bed) in patients selected by post-operative pathology (`pT3` or positive surgical margins).
     - However, the 2012 Lancet 10-year update of the exact same trial (`PMID 23084481`, Row 120) is labeled **`1` (Positive)** in `labeled-dataset-v2.csv`.
   - **Kiel University HDR Brachytherapy + 3D-CRT Series (Laparoscopic Pelvic Lymphadenectomy Exclusion)**:
     - `PMID 15774239` (Row 294, Galalae et al. 2004, $N = 442$, median follow-up 5 years) was previously flagged for overlap between the positive and negative sets and explicitly overridden to **`0` (Negative)** by physician review in `src/build-new-labeled-dataset.py` (`PHYSICIAN_OVERRIDE_NEGATIVE = {15774239}`), because patients underwent laparoscopic pelvic lymph node dissection to pathologically exclude lymph node-positive (`pN+`) patients prior to radiotherapy.
     - However, the 2002 report from the same Kiel group (`PMID 12074814`, Row 5, Galalae et al. 2002, $N = 144$) uses the exact same laparoscopic pelvic lymphadenectomy exclusion protocol and lacks risk-group outcome stratification or a stated median follow-up in the abstract, yet remains labeled **`1` (Positive)**.
4. **Post-Operative Pathologic Staging Selection in Surgical Series (`Label = 1`)**:
   - While Criterion 3 explicitly prohibits post-operative pathologic staging selection and exclusion of unfavorable surgical pathology (such as positive margins), six surgical papers in the positive set (`PMID 11743287`, `PMID 11912386`, `PMID 20350749`, `PMID 27649109`, `PMID 27910728`, and `PMID 28461982`) explicitly define their inclusion cohort by surgical specimen pathology—most notably `PMID 27910728` (*"...following radical prostatectomy for pathological T2 prostate cancer with negative surgical margins"*), which explicitly excludes extraprostatic extension and positive surgical margins.
5. **Near-Threshold Follow-Up Rounding (54 to 59 Months vs. 60 Months)**:
   - Among the positive papers with explicit follow-up $< 60$ months, several cluster tightly between **55 and 59 months** (4.6 to 4.9 years): `PMID 14511029` (mean 55 mo), `PMID 15629591` (median 58.6 mo), `PMID 17162020` (median 59 mo), `PMID 19540066` (median 4.9 yr), `PMID 25614075` (median 57 mo), and `PMID 27276878` (median 56.7 mo). Human reviewers during full-text curation appear to have occasionally rounded ~4.7-4.9 years to 5 years when 5-year Kaplan-Meier actuarial curves were reported, whereas strict adherence to the 60-month median follow-up rule flags these as threshold violations.
6. **Abstract-Level vs. Full-Text Information Gap (`Insufficient Information`, $N = 100$)**:
   - Nearly one-fifth of the dataset (19.08%, including 25.98% of positive papers) cannot be fully verified from the title and abstract alone because authors frequently report 5-, 10-, or 15-year Kaplan-Meier survival curves in the abstract while relegating the exact cohort median follow-up duration, radiation dose in Gy, or per-risk-group patient headcounts to the full-text Methods and Results tables. This explains why abstract-only machine learning classifiers face an irreducible information ceiling when trained or evaluated strictly on abstract text.
